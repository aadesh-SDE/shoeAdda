Usage

Clear space

Keep space equal to the height of the letter S on all sides of the logo.

Minimum size

Icon 24dp. Lockup 96dp wide. Below that, use the icon alone.

Backgrounds

Use the light version on #FBFBFB, the dark version on #131212, and the cocoa version on brand banners.

Do not

Stretch the logo, change its colors, or put ADDA in a different font.
