ShoeAdda color options

Four natural palettes drawn from moss, clay, sage and sea, plus Option E from your four swatches and Option F from the Octet mobile shopping palette. Each keeps a soft earthy base and one accent that is reserved for buttons and selected states, so Add to cart always stands out.

Option ARecommended

Moss and Stone

Deep moss green on a soft stone base. Outdoorsy and calm, with a clean modern edge.

    • ContrastWhite text on accent 6.1:1. Text on base 13.3:1. Dark mode accent 8.8:1.
    • Best forSneakers, walking and trail shoes. The most "natural" feel of the four.
    • RiskGreen can read as eco-brand. Sale tags use a clay red to stay distinct.
    Option B

    Clay and Oat

    Baked clay accent on a warm oat base. Earthy and friendly, like leather and suede.

      • ContrastWhite text on accent 6.2:1. Text on base 14.6:1. Dark mode accent 7.3:1.
      • Best forLeather, formal and casual shoes. Warmest of the four.
      • RiskAccent and sale tag are both warm reds. Keep the sale tag small and the accent on buttons only.
      Option C

        Option D

          Option EYour palette

          Charcoal and Slate

          Your four colors: charcoal #4A4A4A, grey #CBCBCB, butter #FFFFE3 and slate blue #6D8196. Soft, quiet and modern.

            • RolesButter is the background, charcoal is text and buttons, grey is borders and dividers, slate is the accent for selected states and the logo dot. Surface is a slightly darker butter I added.
            • ContrastCharcoal on butter 8.7:1. Butter on charcoal 8.7:1. Slate on butter 4.0:1, fine for icons and borders, too low for small text.
            • FixWhite text on slate is 4.0:1, so buttons use charcoal instead. If you want slate buttons, darken it to #56697D (5.7:1 with white).
            • RiskThe accent is low-key, so Add to cart relies on the charcoal button. Dark mode here is my own derived version.
            Option FOctet palette

            Cocoa and Mist

            From the Octet mobile shopping palette: near-black, cocoa brown, tan, mist blue and soft grey. Earthy and premium.

              • Roles#FBFBFB background, #131212 text, #68513F cocoa for buttons and selected states, #9BB3BE mist blue behind product photos, #A5967C tan for badges and highlights, #D4D5D2 borders. Surface is a tint I added.
              • ContrastWhite on cocoa 7.4:1. Text on background 18.1:1. Near-black on mist blue 8.5:1. Dark mode accent 9.6:1.
              • Watch outTan and mist blue are only 2.1 to 2.8:1 on white, so use them for backgrounds and decoration, never for text or icons.
              • Best forLeather, casual and premium shoes. Photos pop on the blue-grey backdrop.
